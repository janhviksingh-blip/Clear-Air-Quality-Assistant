Health & Safety Guide

Essential Smog Precautions

Key protective habits recommended during high urban pollution and winter smog episodes.

😷 Mask & Eye Shield

Use a snug-fitting N95 / N99 respirator (cloth masks do not block PM2.5). Wear wraparound glasses and rinse eyes with clean water after commuting.

🪟 Smart Ventilation

Keep windows sealed during morning/evening smog peaks. Ventilate briefly between 1 PM – 4 PM when sunlight disperses ground-level pollutants.

🥗 Anti-Inflammatory Diet

Stay hydrated and eat Vitamin C, Omega-3, and antioxidant-rich foods (citrus, walnuts, turmeric, ginger, jaggery) to help combat oxidative stress.

👶 Kids & Seniors Care

Avoid outdoor jogging or playground visits when AQI > 200. Keep prescribed inhalers accessible and avoid burning incense, candles, or waste indoors.
